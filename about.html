About StudyLink

Our Mission

StudyLink was created to connect Caraga State University students seeking academic assistance with peers who excel in various subjects. Our mission is to foster a collaborative learning environment where students can help each other succeed.

Our Vision

To become the go-to platform for CSU students to find reliable peer tutoring support, enhancing academic performance and building a stronger learning community across all disciplines.
